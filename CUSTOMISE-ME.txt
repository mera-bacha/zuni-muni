CUSTOMISE YOUR PROPOSAL

1. Open assets/js/config.js in Notepad or any text editor.
2. Change "Your Name" and "My Love".
3. Change the specialDate using this format:
   2027-02-14T19:00:00
4. Replace the sample messages with your real memories.
5. Put your photos inside assets/images.
6. Replace the sample image filenames in config.js.
7. Save the file and open index.html.

The heart logo contains a hidden surprise. Click it five times.
